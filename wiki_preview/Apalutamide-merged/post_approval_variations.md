# Post-approval variations — apalutamide

This page lists all post-approval variations to the apalutamide marketing authorisations that introduce **new clinical study data**. Variations limited to CMC changes, labeling cosmetics, pharmacovigilance-driven safety labeling additions, or nonclinical updates are listed in the "Excluded" table at the bottom for transparency.

### S-001 — mCSPC indication extension (TITAN)

- **Source verbatim (S-001 letter):** "This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for expanding the indication to include the use of Erleada® for treatment of patients with metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer (mCSPC)."
  - [[sources/suppl-001-210951orig1s001ltr-hybrid|FDA Supplement S-001 Approval Letter]] [NDA-210951-S001-LTR | Approval Letter | p.1]
- **Clinical evidence base:** TITAN Phase 3 (1052 patients, rPFS HR 0.50, OS HR 0.69) — see [[studies/clinical/nct02489318]].

### S-004 — SPARTAN final OS PMC fulfillment

- **Source verbatim (S-004 letter):** "This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for labeling revisions to the U.S. prescribing information based on results from the final analysis of overall survival, efficacy and safety from the SPARTAN study ARN-509-003."
  - [[sources/suppl-004-210951orig1s004ltr-hybrid|FDA Supplement S-004 Approval Letter]] [NDA-210951-S004-LTR | Approval Letter | p.1]
- **Final OS results:** HR 0.78 (95% CI 0.64, 0.96), p=0.0161; median OS 73.9 vs 59.9 months. See [[studies/clinical/nct01946204|SPARTAN]] §Final OS Analysis.

### S-006 — TITAN final OS PMC fulfillment

- **Source verbatim (S-006 letter):** "This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for updates in the U.S. prescribing information for Erleada (apalutamide) to reflect the analysis from the final report for Overall Survival (OS) of the TITAN trial."
  - [[sources/suppl-006-210951orig1s006ltr-hybrid|FDA Supplement S-006 Approval Letter]] [NDA-210951-S006-LTR | Approval Letter | p.1]
- **Clinical evidence:** TITAN longer-follow-up OS data. See [[studies/clinical/nct02489318|TITAN]].

### S-021 — Severe hepatic impairment new study

- **Source verbatim (S-021 letter):** "This Prior Approval sNDA provides for the addition of hepatic impairment data to Section 2.3 Recommended Dosage in Patients with Severe Hepatic Impairment..."
  - [[sources/suppl-021-210951orig1s021ltr-hybrid|FDA Supplement S-021 Approval Letter]] [NDA-210951-S021-LTR | Approval Letter | p.1]
- **Clinical evidence:** Dedicated Child-Pugh C study; AUC 2.1-fold increase; revised dose 120 mg QD. See [[evidence/special_populations]] §Severe hepatic impairment.

### EMA Variation II/0001 — full TITAN dossier

- **Source verbatim:** "Extension of Indication to include the treatment of metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC) in combination with androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) for Erleada based on the results of study 56021927PCR3002 (TITAN study), a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 study comparing apalutamide plus ADT versus ADT in patients with mHSPC"
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 1.1 Type II variation | p.5]
- **Result:** TITAN Phase 3 became the primary supporting evidence for the EU mHSPC indication. The variation also added a SmPC §4.4 warning on **ischaemic cardiovascular events** observed at higher incidence with apalutamide+ADT vs ADT alone.

### EMA Extension X/0028/G — 240 mg strength addition

- **Source verbatim:** "Janssen-Cilag International N.V. submitted on 9 November 2022 a group of variations consisting of an extension application to add a new strength (240 mg) film-coated tablets of the marketing authorisation"
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 1.1 Background | p.5]
- **Two new BE studies supporting:** Both were post-approval, in healthy volunteers, designed to bridge the new 240 mg single-tablet formulation (G043) to the marketed 60 mg formulation (G023).
  - **PCR1027** — formulation selection study; investigated relative bioavailability of multiple 240 mg FC tablet formulations vs commercial 60 mg G023 under fasting conditions. Selected G043 for pivotal evaluation.
  - **PCR1028** — pivotal BE study; G043 (240 mg) vs G023 (4×60 mg) BE assessment + fed/fasted BA of G043.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 1.1 Clinical Development | p.6]
  - Verbatim: "The following two clinical studies support the new FC tablet formulation: · Study 56021927PCR1027, a formulation selection study which investigated the relative Bioavailability (BA) of 240 mg FC tablet formulations compared with the current commercial FC tablets (G023) under fasting conditions."
- **Note on user-defined inclusion criterion:** The user's filter was "only variations based on new clinical studies". PCR1027/1028 ARE new clinical studies (BE/BA studies in healthy volunteers); they are included here per literal application. They are BE-type studies (not efficacy or safety studies), so depending on what counts as "clinical study", they may be a borderline inclusion. Flagged here for the user to review.
