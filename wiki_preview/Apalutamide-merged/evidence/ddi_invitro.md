# Drug interactions — in vitro — apalutamide

## Enzyme inhibition (apalutamide as inhibitor)

- **CYP2C8 (apalutamide):** IC50 = 18 µM (in vitro).
- **CYP2B6/2C9/2C19/3A4 (apalutamide):** IC50 range 36–67 µM (in vitro inhibition).
- **CYP2B6/2C8/2C9/2C19/3A4 (N-desmethyl apalutamide):** IC50 range 38–75 µM.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — In Vitro CYP Inhibition | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide was an inhibitor of CYP2C8 (IC50 = 18 uM). It also was identified as an inhibitor of CYP2B6, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, and CYP3A4 with IC50 range from 36 to 67 uM."

## Enzyme induction (apalutamide as inducer)

- **CYP3A4, CYP2B6 (in vitro):** Both apalutamide and N-desmethyl apalutamide are inducers.
- **CYP3A4, CYP2C19 (in vivo):** Apalutamide is a strong inducer.
- **CYP2C9 (in vivo):** Apalutamide is a weak inducer.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Induction | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "In vitro, apalutamide and N-desmethyl apalutamide were identified as CYP3A4 and CYP2B6 inducers."

## Transporter inhibition

- **P-gp (apalutamide / N-desmethyl):** Weak inhibitors — 56% / 42% inhibition of digoxin transport at 30 µM.
- **BCRP (apalutamide / N-desmethyl):** Weak inhibitors — 45% / 33% inhibition of topotecan transport at 30 µM.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Transporter In Vitro | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "weak inhibitors of P-gp with inhibition of digoxin transport (56% for apalutamide and 42% for N-desmethyl apalutamide at 30 µM) and BCRP with inhibition of BCRP-mediated topotecan transport (inhibition of 45% and 33%, respectively, at 30 µM)."
- **OCT2 (apalutamide / N-desmethyl):** IC50 27.2 / 4.8 µM.
- **OAT3:** 12.0 / 7.5 µM.
- **MATE1:** 13.8 / 17.6 µM.
- **MATE-2K:** 37.9 µM (apa) / 32% inhibition at 50 µM (N-desmethyl).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Renal Transporters | p.74]
  - Verbatim: "In vitro, apalutamide showed inhibition of OCT2 (IC50 = 27.2 μ.Μ), ΟΑΤ3 (IC50 = 12.0 μM) and MATE1 (IC50 = 13.8 μM) and ΜΑΤΕ-2Κ (IC50 = 37.9 μ.Μ)"
- **OAT3 (apalutamide steady-state 240 mg QD):** Not expected to clinically affect benzylpenicillin exposure (per FDA 2017 DDI guidance prediction).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — OAT3 prediction | p.74]
  - Verbatim: "steady-state apalutamide (240 mg once daily) is not expected to have a clinically relevant effect on the exposure to benzylpenicillin"
