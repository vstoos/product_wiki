# Drug interactions — clinical — apalutamide

All studies below from FDA Multidisciplinary Review Section 6.3.2, conducted in healthy volunteers unless noted. PBPK predictions are flagged as such.

## Effect of perpetrators on apalutamide (apalutamide as victim)

- **Itraconazole (strong CYP3A4 inhibitor) — single-dose apalutamide:** Apalutamide Cmax −22%, no AUC change. N-desmethyl Cmax −15%, no AUC change. Dedicated study 1012.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — DDI Inhibitors | p.72]
  - Verbatim: "In a dedicated drug-interaction study 1012, concomitant itraconazole (a strong CYP3A4 inhibitor) decreased a single dose apalutamide's Cmax by 22 % without AUC change."
- **Ketoconazole (strong CYP3A4 inhibitor, PBPK prediction):** Apalutamide steady-state Cmax +38%, AUC0-24 +51%. Active unbound fraction (apa + 1/3 × N-desmethyl) Cmax +23%, AUC0-24 +28%. No initial dose adjustment recommended.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — DDI PBPK ketoconazole | p.72]
  - Verbatim: "physiologically-based PK (PBPK) simulation predicted that 400 mg qd ketoconazole, a strong CYP3A4 inhibitors, increased apalutamide Cmax and AUC0-24 at steady state by 38% and 51%."
- **Gemfibrozil (strong CYP2C8 inhibitor) — single-dose apalutamide:** Apalutamide Cmax −21%, AUC0-672 +53%, AUCinf +68%. N-desmethyl Cmax −45%, AUC0-672 −43%, AUCinf −15%. Study 1012.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — DDI CYP2C8 inhibitor | p.72]
  - Verbatim: "concomitant 600 mg gemfibrozil (a strong CYP2C8 inhibitor) bid decreased a single dose apalutamide's Cmax by 21% while AUC0-672 and AUCinf increased by 53% and 68%, respectively."
- **Gemfibrozil steady-state PBPK prediction:** Apalutamide Cmax +32%, AUC0-24 +44% at 240 mg QD coadministration. Active unbound fraction Cmax +19%, AUC0-24 +23%.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — DDI PBPK gemfibrozil ss | p.72]
  - Verbatim: "apalutamide Cmax and AUC0-24 increased by 32% and 44%, respectively; active unbound fraction Cmax and AUC0-24 increased by 19% and 23%"
- **Rifampin (strong CYP3A4 / moderate CYP2C8 inducer, PBPK prediction):** Apalutamide steady-state Cmax −25%, AUC0-24 −34%. Active unbound fraction Cmax −15%, AUC0-24 −19%. No dose adjustment.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — DDI Inducers PBPK | p.73]
  - Verbatim: "coadministration of 600 mg qd rifampin, a strong CYP3A4 and moderate 2C8 inducer, decreased the apalutamide steady-state Cmax and AUC0-24 by 25% and 34%, respectively."

## Effect of apalutamide on CYP substrates (apalutamide as perpetrator)

- **Midazolam (CYP3A4 substrate):** AUC −92% with multiple 240 mg apalutamide. Recommendation: substitute or evaluate efficacy loss.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — DDI CYP3A4 substrate | p.73]
  - Verbatim: "In study 1020, coadministration of midazolam, a CYP3A4 substrate, with multiple daily doses of 240 mg apalutamide decreased the midazolam AUC by 92%."
- **Omeprazole (CYP2C19 substrate):** AUC −85%. Substitute or evaluate efficacy loss.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — DDI CYP2C19 substrate | p.73]
  - Verbatim: "coadministration of omeprazole, a CYP2C19 substrate, with multiple daily doses of 240 mg apalutamide decreased the omeprazole AUC by 85%."
- **S-warfarin (CYP2C9 substrate):** AUC −46%. Substitute or, if coadministration unavoidable, monitor INR.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — DDI CYP2C9 substrate | p.73]
  - Verbatim: "coadministration of S-warfarin, a CYP2C9 substrate, with multiple daily doses of 240 mg apalutamide decreased the S-warfarin AUC by 46%."
- **Pioglitazone (CYP2C8 substrate):** AUC −18%. No dose adjustment.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — DDI CYP2C8 substrate | p.73]
  - Verbatim: "coadministration of pioglitazone, a CYP2C8 substrate, with multiple daily doses of 240 mg apalutamide decreased the pioglitazone AUC by 18%."

## Effect on transporter substrates

- **Fexofenadine (P-gp substrate):** AUC −30%. Use caution.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Transporter DDI | p.74]
  - Verbatim: "coadministration of fexofenadine, a P-gp substrate, with multiple daily doses of 240 mg apalutamide decreased the fexofenadine AUC by 30%."
- **Rosuvastatin (BCRP/OATP1B1 substrate):** AUC −41%. Use caution.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Transporter DDI | p.74]
  - Verbatim: "coadministration of rosuvastatin, a BCRP/OATP1B1 substrate, with multiple daily doses of 240 mg apalutamide decreased the rosuvastatin AUC by 41%."

## Acid-lowering agents

- **PPI / H2RA / antacids:** Not expected to alter apalutamide bioavailability (apalutamide is non-ionizable at physiological pH).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Acid-lowering agents | p.72]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide is not ionizable under relevant physiological pH condition. Acid lowering agents (e.g. proton pump inhibitor, H2-receptor antacid) are not expected to affect the bioavailability of apalutamide."

- **Itraconazole DDI (alternative metric — inactive carboxylic acid metabolite):** +44% AUC of the carboxylic acid metabolite; parent +1% AUC; N-desmethyl +12% AUC.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Metabolism / DDI | p.44]
  - Verbatim: "the CYP3A4 inhibitor itraconazol (1% increase in apalutamide AUC, 12% increase in N-desmethyl apalutamide and 44% increase in inactive carboxylic acid metabolite AUC)"
- **Gemfibrozil DDI (CHMP-presented values):** +68% apalutamide AUC, −15.2% N-desmethyl apalutamide AUC, +102% inactive carboxylic acid metabolite AUC.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Metabolism / DDI | p.44]
  - Verbatim: "the CYP2C8 inhibitor gemfibrozil (68% increase in apalutamide AUC, 15.2% decrease in N-desmethyl apalutamide and 102% increase in inactive carboxylic acid metabolite AUC)"
- **Metabolism shift PBPK caveat:** CHMP notes the CYP2C8→CYP3A4 contribution shift (58%/13% single-dose → 40%/37% steady-state) "can only be considered as an exploratory model. Thus, firm conclusion from it should not be reached and results should be interpreted with caution."
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Metabolism | p.44]

## Leuprolide DDI sub-study (TITAN, post-approval)

A pre-planned PK substudy within TITAN (501 patients with mHSPC, two arms: placebo+leuprolide and apalutamide+leuprolide) evaluated whether apalutamide alters the PK/PD of leuprolide acetate (the ADT backbone, a GnRH analogue).

- **Leuprolide exposure 42% higher in apalutamide+leuprolide arm** — driven by higher baseline leuprolide (pre-apalutamide), not a metabolic DDI.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.4.4 Discussion on Clinical Pharmacology | p.~22]
  - Verbatim: "Higher exposure of leuprolide (42%) was observed in the apalutamide+leuprolide arm versus placebo+leuprolide arm. This was caused by the higher exposure of leuprolide in patients at baseline (pre-treatment with apalutamide)."
- **CHMP conclusion:** No clinically meaningful effect of apalutamide on steady-state leuprolide PK/PD; no dose adjustment for leuprolide needed when coadministered with apalutamide.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.4.4 Discussion on Clinical Pharmacology | p.~22]
  - Verbatim: "co-administration with apalutamide had no apparent effect on the steady-state exposure of leuprolide in mHSPC subjects receiving leuprolide acetate (a GnRH analog) (see SmPC section 4.5)."
