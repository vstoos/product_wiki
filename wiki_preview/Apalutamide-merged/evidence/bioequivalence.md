# Bioequivalence — apalutamide

## Tablet vs capsule relative BE — Study 1011

- **Apalutamide tablet vs capsule Cmax (GMR, 90% CI):** 0.90 (0.79, 1.03) — fails strict 80–125% BE on Cmax lower bound; flagged as "almost BE" by FDA reviewer.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — BE Tablet/Capsule | p.65]
  - Verbatim: "Bioequivalent (BE) tablets/capsule GMR (90% CI) | Almost BE | 0.90 (0.79, 1.03) | 1.08 (0.95, 1.23) | 1.07 (0.94, 1.22)"
- **Apalutamide tablet vs capsule AUClast (GMR, 90% CI):** 1.08 (0.95, 1.23) — within 80–125%.
- **Apalutamide tablet vs capsule AUCinf (GMR, 90% CI):** 1.07 (0.94, 1.22) — within 80–125%.
  - Same source row.
- **Clinical conclusion:** No clinically relevant exposure differences between the two formulations.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Biopharmaceutics — BE Tablet vs Capsule | p.9]
  - Verbatim: "based on the BA study PCR1011 results, there is no clinically relevant exposure differences between these two formulations"

## Generic BE study design recommendation (FDA PSG)

The FDA Product-Specific Guidance (PSG_210951) recommends:

- **Two in vivo BE studies:** one fasting, one fed.
  - [[sources/psg-210951-hybrid|FDA PSG Apalutamide]] [PSG-210951 | Product-Specific Guidance | Header | p.1]
  - Verbatim: "Recommended Studies: | Two in vivo bioequivalence studies with pharmacokinetic endpoints"
- **Design (default):** Single-dose, two-treatment, two-period crossover, 240 mg strength, healthy males.
  - [[sources/psg-210951-hybrid|FDA PSG Apalutamide]] [PSG-210951 | Product-Specific Guidance | Study #1 Fasting | p.1]
  - Verbatim: "Design: Single-dose, two-treatment, two-period crossover in vivo Strength: 240 mg Subjects: Healthy males"
- **Long half-life caveat — parallel-design option:** "Apalutamide has a long terminal elimination half-life. Ensure adequate washout periods between treatments in a crossover study or consider using a parallel study design."
  - [[sources/psg-210951-hybrid|FDA PSG Apalutamide]] [PSG-210951 | Product-Specific Guidance | Additional Comments | p.1]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide has a long terminal elimination half-life. Ensure adequate washout periods between treatments in a crossover study or consider using a parallel study design."
- **BE acceptance basis:** 90% CI of GMR for apalutamide in plasma.
  - [[sources/psg-210951-hybrid|FDA PSG Apalutamide]] [PSG-210951 | Product-Specific Guidance | BE basis | p.2]
  - Verbatim: "Bioequivalence based on (90% CI): Apalutamide"
- **60 mg strength waiver:** Granted based on (i) acceptable 240 mg BE, (ii) acceptable comparative dissolution, (iii) proportional formulation similarity.
  - [[sources/psg-210951-hybrid|FDA PSG Apalutamide]] [PSG-210951 | Product-Specific Guidance | Waiver | p.2]
  - Verbatim: "Waiver request of in vivo testing: 60 mg strength based on (i) acceptable bioequivalence studies on the 240 mg strength, (ii) acceptable in vitro dissolution testing of both strengths, and (iii) proportional similarity of the formulations between both strengths"
- **Feeding-tube studies (in vitro):** Required because the 240 mg tablet may be administered via NG/G tube per RLD labeling.
  - [[sources/psg-210951-hybrid|FDA PSG Apalutamide]] [PSG-210951 | Product-Specific Guidance | Feeding tube | p.2]
  - Verbatim: "The approved labeling for the RLD states that the 240 mg tablet may be administered through a feeding tube. Conduct the in vitro feeding tube studies"

See [[understanding/apalutamide-bioequivalence]] for design discussion (crossover vs parallel given the ~3-day effective half-life) and [[understanding/apalutamide-pk-variability]] for sample-size implications.

## EMA EPAR multi-source confirmation

The EMA EPAR (Section 2.4.2 BIOEQUIVALENCE, p.42–43) provides additional formulation context that the FDA review summarizes more briefly:

- **Study 1011 had FOUR treatment arms** (not the two described in the FDA summary):
  - Treatment A: 240 mg as 8×30 mg softgel capsules (reference)
  - Treatment B: 240 mg as 4×60 mg **SDP (HPMC-AS ratio 1:3) tablets** — the to-be-marketed formulation
  - Treatment C: 240 mg as 4×60 mg **HME (HPMC-AS ratio 1:3) tablets**
  - Treatment D: 240 mg as 4×60 mg **SDP (Eudragit ratio 1:2) tablets**
  - Verbatim: "A: 240 mg given as 8x30 mg softgel capsules B: 240 mg dose given as 4x60 mg SDP (HPMC-AS ratio 1:3) tablets C: 240 mg dose given as 4x60 mg HME (HPMC-AS ratio 1:3) tablets D: 240 mg dose given as 4x60 mg SDP (Eudragit ratio 1:2) tablets"
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Bioequivalence | p.42]
- **Repeat-dose tablet/capsule BE substudy (Study 1010, N=4 mCRPC patients):** Cmax GMR 92.05% (90% CI 66.66–127.12%), AUC0-24 GMR 94.30% (90% CI 82.19–108.20%). Wide CIs reflect the small N.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Bioequivalence | p.42]
  - Verbatim: "Following repeat dose administrations in subjects with mCRPC (n=4), the tablet versus capsule GMRs were 92.05% for Cmax (90% CI: 66.66-127.12%) and 94.30% for AUC0-24 (90% CI: 82.19-108.20%) (Substudy 1010)."
- **CHMP regulatory framing:** "The formulations are not strictly bioequivalent according to the criteria outlined in the Bioequivalence guideline. ... these criteria have, however, been set with generic applications in mind." CHMP accepted the originator's formulation transition based on additional safety/efficacy evidence and the parallel food-effect substudy.
- **Studies 1015 and 1017** (relative BA across CQA and manufacturing-site variants of the final tablet) **met strict BE criteria** for both Cmax and AUC.
  - Verbatim: "Results of those studies showed that formulations were bioequivalent according to the criteria outlined in the Bioequivalence guideline."
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Bioequivalence | p.43]

## Post-approval BE studies — 240 mg single-tablet strength (G043 vs G023)

Two new clinical pharmacology studies submitted with EMA Extension X/0028/G (2023) and FDA Supplement S-011 (2022) to support the introduction of the 240 mg single-tablet film-coated formulation (G043). Both studies in healthy adult male participants.

### PCR1027 (formulation selection — relative BA)

Single-dose, open-label, 2-way crossover. Part 1 evaluated relative BA of 1×240 mg G043 (Test, Treatment C) vs 4×60 mg G023 (Reference, Treatment A) under fasting. N=13 per treatment.

| PK parameter | Reference (G023) geomean | Test (G043) geomean | GMR (%) | 90% CI (%) | Intra-participant CV (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cmax (µg/mL) | 1.95 | 2.25 | **115.2** | **101.16–131.20** | **18.6** |
| AUC0-72h (h·µg/mL) | 54.7 | 55.8 | 102.02 | 99.05–105.07 | 4.2 |

- **Conclusion (PCR1027):** Cmax 90% CI upper bound (131.20) **exceeded** the 125.00% BE limit. G043 was **not strictly bioequivalent** to G023 in this formulation-selection study — formulation refinement followed, then the pivotal BE was re-run as PCR1028.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 2.6.3 — Study 56021927PCR1027 Results Table 2 | p.21]
  - Verbatim: "Cmax (ug/mL) | 1.95 | 2.25 | 115.2 | 101.16-131.20 | 18.6"

### PCR1028 — pivotal bioequivalence (Part 1, fasting)

Randomized, open-label, single-dose, single-center, 2-period 2-way crossover. N=74 randomized (2 sequences × 37 each); analysis populations N=65 for Cmax, N=63 for AUC0-72h. Minimum 7-week washout (designed for apalutamide's long half-life).

| PK parameter | Reference (4×60 mg G023) | Test (1×240 mg G043) | GMR (%) | 90% CI (%) | **Intra-participant CV (%)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cmax (µg/mL) | 2.05 | 2.25 | 109.67 | 104.55–115.04 | **16.4** |
| AUC0-72h (h·µg/mL) | 57.7 | 59.3 | 102.71 | 100.78–104.68 | **6.4** |

- **Conclusion (PCR1028 Part 1):** **G043 is bioequivalent to G023.** Both 90% CIs fall fully within the 80.00%–125.00% acceptance window.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 2.6.3 — Study 56021927PCR1028 Part 1 Table 5 | p.28]
  - Verbatim: "Cmax (ug/mL) | 2.05 | 2.25 | 109.67 | 104.55-115.04 | 16.4"
- **Source CHMP conclusion:** "Treatment B (G043) is bioequivalent to Treatment A (G023). The 90% CI of the GMR of Cmax and AUC0-72h fell completely within the BE limits of 80.00%-125.00%."
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 2.6.3 — PK conclusion | p.29]

### PCR1028 — food effect on 240 mg G043 (Part 2)

Same design, 2-way crossover N=20, fed (Test, D) vs fasted (Reference, C) — both arms received the 240 mg G043 single tablet.

| PK parameter | Reference (fasted G043) | Test (fed G043) | GMR (%) | 90% CI (%) | **Intra-participant CV (%)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cmax (µg/mL) | 2.36 | 2.15 | 90.96 | 83.06–99.61 | **16.7** |
| AUC0-72h (h·µg/mL) | 59.2 | 59.2 | 99.94 | 97.12–102.84 | **5.2** |

- **Conclusion (PCR1028 Part 2):** No clinically relevant food effect on the 240 mg G043 tablet. 90% CI of Cmax GMR fell within 80–125% (lower bound 83.06%, vs the older G023 4-tablet study 1011 lower bound 74.90%) — slightly attenuated food effect on Cmax with the new single-tablet form.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 2.6.3 — Study 56021927PCR1028 Part 2 Table 6 | p.28]
  - Verbatim: "C<sub>max</sub> (&mu;g/mL) | 2.36 | 2.15 | 90.96 | 83.06-99.61 | 16.7"

### Key observation: variability is LOWER in the modern BE study

The PCR1028 pivotal BE study (N=65, 2022 conduct) measured **intra-subject CV%** directly via the standard mixed-model approach:

- **Cmax intra-CV: 16–17%** — consistent across Parts 1 (fasting BE) and 2 (food effect); similar to original PCR1027 (18.6%).
- **AUC0-72h intra-CV: 5–6%** — substantially lower than the original PCR1027 (Part 1 AUC intra-CV 4.2%) and **much lower than my earlier back-calculation** from the N=12 capsule food-effect substudy in Study 001 (~10% AUC).

The directly-measured intra-CV from a 65-subject pivotal crossover is the higher-confidence value. See [[understanding/apalutamide-pk-variability]] for the revised sample-size estimates that incorporate these measured values.
