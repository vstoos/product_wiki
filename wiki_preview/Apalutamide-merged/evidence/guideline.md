# Guideline (FDA PSG) — apalutamide

The FDA Product-Specific Guidance for apalutamide (PSG_210951, recommended May 2019, revised August 2024) sets the bioequivalence and dissolution recipe for generic development.

- **Active / dosage form / route / strengths:** Apalutamide tablet, oral, 60 mg and 240 mg.
  - [[sources/psg-210951-hybrid|FDA PSG Apalutamide]] [PSG-210951 | Product-Specific Guidance | Header | p.1]
  - Verbatim: "Active Ingredient: | Apalutamide | Dosage Form: | Tablet | Route: | Oral | Strengths: | 60 mg, 240 mg"
- **Recommended studies:** Two in vivo BE studies with PK endpoints (fasting + fed).
  - Verbatim: "Recommended Studies: | Two in vivo bioequivalence studies with pharmacokinetic endpoints"
- **Design (default):** Single-dose, two-treatment, two-period crossover; 240 mg strength; healthy males.
  - Verbatim: "Design: Single-dose, two-treatment, two-period crossover in vivo Strength: 240 mg Subjects: Healthy males"
- **Long-half-life caveat:** Adequate washout in crossover OR consider parallel design.
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide has a long terminal elimination half-life. Ensure adequate washout periods between treatments in a crossover study or consider using a parallel study design."
- **Analyte / BE basis:** Apalutamide in plasma; 90% CI of GMR.
  - Verbatim: "Analyte to measure: Apalutamide in plasma" and "Bioequivalence based on (90% CI): Apalutamide"
- **60 mg waiver criteria:** Acceptable 240 mg BE + dissolution comparability + formulation proportionality.
  - Verbatim: "Waiver request of in vivo testing: 60 mg strength based on (i) acceptable bioequivalence studies on the 240 mg strength, (ii) acceptable in vitro dissolution testing of both strengths, and (iii) proportional similarity of the formulations between both strengths"
- **Dissolution method:** Refer to FDA Dissolution Methods database; conduct comparative dissolution on 12 units of each strength of test and reference.
  - Verbatim: "Conduct comparative dissolution testing on 12 dosage units each of both strengths of the test and reference products."
- **Feeding-tube in vitro studies:** Required (8 Fr NG and 12 Fr G tubes; multiple tube materials/designs; G tube with inflated balloon; holding times 0 and 15 min; record pH of water).
  - Verbatim: "Testing tube: Nasogastric tube (8 French) and gastrostomy (G) tube (12 French) 1. Three types of tube configurations including different tube materials"
