# Solubility — apalutamide

## Aqueous solubility

- **Aqueous media (pH 1–11):** Practically insoluble across a wide pH range.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Chemistry/Product Quality Review | Drug Substance | p.7]
  - Verbatim: "The drug substance is practically insoluble in aqueous media over a wide range of pH values"
- **Aqueous media confirmation:** Practically insoluble in aqueous media.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Chemistry/Product Quality Review | Description | p.12]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide is practically insoluble in aqueous media over a wide range of pH values."
- **Strong base (pH > 11):** Chemically unstable.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Chemistry/Product Quality Review | Drug Substance | p.7]
  - Verbatim: "The solubility experiments show that it is unstable to strong base (pH >11)"

## Organic solvents

- **Range across organic solvents:** Practically insoluble to very soluble.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Chemistry/Product Quality Review | Drug Substance | p.7]
  - Verbatim: "practically insoluble to very soluble in organic solvents"

## BCS-relevant context

Apalutamide is a BCS Class 2 compound (low solubility, high permeability) per the applicant's CMC submission — see [[understanding/apalutamide-bcs-classification]].

## Quantitative aqueous solubility (CHMP value)

- **Aqueous solubility:** 0.001 g/100 mL = 0.01 mg/mL = 2.5 mg in 250 mL.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Absorption | p.42]
  - Verbatim: "low aqueous solubility (0.001 g/100 mL or 2.5 mg/250 mL), apalutamide can be considered a BCS class II substance"
- **BCS classification implication:** At the 240 mg maximum daily dose, 2.5 mg dissolves in 250 mL — i.e. only ~1% of the dose can dissolve in 250 mL of aqueous medium. **Confirms BCS Class 2** (low solubility, high permeability).
