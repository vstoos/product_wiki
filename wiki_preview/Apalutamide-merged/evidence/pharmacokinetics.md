# Pharmacokinetics — apalutamide

## Absorption

- **Absolute oral bioavailability:** About 100%.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Summary Table | p.65]
  - Verbatim: "Oral Bioavailability | Absolute oral bioavailability about 100%"
- **Tmax (apalutamide):** Median 2 hours (range 1–5 hours) after single 240 mg dose.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Summary Table | p.65]
  - Verbatim: "Oral Tmax | The Tmax was 2 hours (range 1 to 5 hours)"
- **Dose proportionality:** Dose-proportional exposure across 30–480 mg.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Summary Table | p.65]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide showed dose-proportional increase in exposure with increasing doses across the dose range from 30 to 480 mg."
- **Accumulation at 240 mg QD:** Apalutamide AUC accumulation ratio ~5; N-desmethyl-apalutamide accumulation ratio ~122.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Summary Table | p.65]
  - Verbatim: "At 240 mg once daily dosing steady-state, the mean AUC accumulation ratio for apalutamide and N- desmethyl apalutamide was 5 and 122, respectively."

## Distribution

- **Vss/F (apparent, steady-state):** Apalutamide 276 L; N-desmethyl apalutamide 236 L.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Summary Table | p.65]
  - Verbatim: "apparent volume of distribution at steady-state (Vss/F) is 276 L and 236 L for apalutamide and N-desmethyl apalutamide, respectively."
- **Plasma protein binding:** Apalutamide 96%; N-desmethyl apalutamide 95%.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Summary Table | p.65]
  - Verbatim: "Plasma Protein | Apalutamide 96%; N-desmethyl apalutamide 95%."
- **Blood-to-plasma ratio:** Apalutamide 0.79; N-desmethyl apalutamide 0.78.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Summary Table | p.65]
  - Verbatim: "Blood to Plasma Ratio | Apalutamide 0.79; N-desmethyl apalutamide 0.78."

## Elimination

- **Effective half-life at steady state:** Apalutamide ~3 days.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Summary Table | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide effective half-life at steady-state 3 days"
- **Clearance (CL/F):** Apalutamide 2 L/h at steady state, 1.3 L/h after single dose. N-desmethyl apalutamide 1.5 L/h.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Summary Table | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide CL/F: at steady-state 2 L/hr; after single dose 1.3 L/hr. N-desmethyl apalutamide CL/F: 1.5 L/hr."

## Metabolism

- **Primary metabolic pathway:** Metabolism is the main route of elimination; apalutamide is primarily metabolized by CYP2C8 and CYP3A4 to N-desmethyl apalutamide; carboxylesterase also contributes.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Metabolism | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide primarily metabolized by CYP2C8 and CYP3A4 to form N-desmethyl apalutamide."
- **CYP2C8 / CYP3A4 contribution shift with CYP3A4 auto-induction:** Single-dose contribution CYP2C8 58% / CYP3A4 13%; steady-state 240 mg QD CYP2C8 40% / CYP3A4 37%.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Metabolism | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "The contribution of CYP2C8 and CYP3A4 in the metabolism of apalutamide is estimated to be 58% and 13% following single dose but changes to 40% and 37%, respectively at steady-state due to CYP3A4 auto-induction."

## Excretion

- **Mass balance (radioactivity recovery):** Urine 65% (1.2% as apalutamide, 2.7% as N-desmethyl); feces 24% (1.5% as apalutamide, 2% as N-desmethyl).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Excretion | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "radioactivity recoveries of dose in urine and feces were 65% (1.2% as apalutamide and 2.7% as N-desmethyl apalutamide) and 24% (1.5% as apalutamide and 2% as N-desmethyl apalutamide), respectively."

## Variability (total CV%, steady-state population PK)

- **Apalutamide Cmax CV%:** 28% at 240 mg QD steady-state (CRPC patients).
- **Apalutamide AUC0-24,ss CV%:** 32% at 240 mg QD steady-state.
- **N-desmethyl Cmax CV%:** 18%; AUC0-24,ss CV% 19%.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Summary Table — Variability | p.65]
  - Verbatim: "CV% for Cmax and AUC0-24,ss In patients with CRPC after 240 mg multiple doses Apalutamide: 28% (Cmax) and 32% (AUC0-24,55). N-desmethyl apalutamide: 18% (Cmax) and 19% (AUCo-24,ss)."

See [[understanding/apalutamide-pk-variability]] for inter- vs intra-subject decomposition and back-calculation from BE confidence intervals.

## QT/QTc

- **Maximum mean ΔQTcF:** 12.4 ms (2-sided 90% upper CI 16.1 ms) at 240 mg QD in 45 CRPC patients (open-label, uncontrolled QT study PCR1019).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — QT Prolongation | p.68]
  - Verbatim: "The maximum mean QTcF change from baseline was 12.4 ms (2-sided 90% upper CI: 16.1 ms)."
- **Exposure-QT relationship:** Concentration-dependent QTcF increase for both apalutamide and N-desmethyl apalutamide.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — QT Prolongation | p.68]
  - Verbatim: "An exposure-QT analysis suggested a concentration-dependent increase in QTcF for apalutamide and its active metabolite"

## EMA EPAR multi-source confirmation

- **Effective half-life at steady state (CHMP value):** ~78.7 h (≈3 days) at steady-state, vs single-dose t1/2 of 183 hours.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Elimination | p.47]
  - Verbatim: "The mean EHL for apalutamide in subjects with CRPC was about 78.7 hours (approximately 3 days) at steady-state"
- **Absolute oral bioavailability (CHMP value, dose-normalized):** Fabs = 1.1 (range 1.08–1.13). Confirms FDA's "≈100%".
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Absorption | p.42]
  - Verbatim: "Absorption of apalutamide is high based oral bioavailability study 006 (after dose normalization to 240 mg, Fabs=1.1 and ranged from 1.08 to 1.13 between subjects)"
- **In vitro permeability (Caco-2 A-to-B):** Papp = 42.3 × 10⁻⁶ cm/s — supports BCS Class 2 high-permeability classification.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Absorption | p.42]
  - Verbatim: "The in vitro permeability of MDV3100 is high (Papp A-to-B of 42.3x10-6 cm/s)"
- **Mass balance recovery:** Urine 64.6% + faeces 22.8% of dose (matching FDA values within rounding).
- **N-desmethyl apalutamide contribution to clinical activity:** ~28–32% (CHMP estimate) — one-third potency of parent, higher AUC (metabolite/parent AUC ratio 1.3, CV 21%), lower protein binding (95% vs 96%).
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Introduction | p.41]
  - Verbatim: "is thus expected to contribute substantially to drug effect (about 28% to 32% of the clinical activity of apalutamide)"
- **Transporter handling:** Apalutamide and N-desmethyl are P-gp substrates but **NOT** substrates of BCRP, OATP1B1, or OATP1B3.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Absorption | p.42]
  - Verbatim: "apalutamide and N-desmethyl apalutamide are substrates of P-gp but not substrates of BCRP, OATP1B1, or OATP1B3"
- **Blood-to-plasma ratio of total ¹⁴C-radioactivity:** 0.91–0.98 (study 006) — confirms FDA single-substance ratio of 0.79 for parent.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Distribution | p.44]
  - Verbatim: "The mean blood-to-plasma ratios of total 14C-radioactivity ranged from 0.91 to 0.98"
- **Time to steady state:** Achieved after 4 weeks of once-daily dosing (Study 001).
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Dose proportionality | p.46]
  - Verbatim: "apalutamide steady state was achieved after 4 weeks (Study 001)"

## Japanese-population PK (PMDA Review Report)

PMDA approved Erleada on **February 18, 2019** based on review of the original NDA dataset plus 2 dedicated Japanese clinical studies.

### Japanese healthy-adult single-dose PK — Study 21 (CTD 5.3.3.1.1; PCR1021; Oct–Dec 2016)

Open-label, randomized single-dose dose-escalation in 18 Japanese healthy adults using the 60 mg FC tablet (apalutamide 60, 120, or 240 mg given as 1, 2, or 4 tablets).

| Dose (mg) | n | Apalutamide Cmax (µg/mL, mean ± SD) | tmax median (h) | Apalutamide AUCinf (µg·h/mL) | t1/2 (h) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | 6 | 0.870 ± 0.192 | 2.50 (1.50–4.00) | 63.9 ± 13.1 | 138 ± 62.0 |
| 120 | 6 | 1.73 ± 0.285 | 1.75 (1.00–3.00) | 147 ± 36.7 | 169 ± 54.0 |
| 240 | 6 | **3.12 ± 0.745** | 3.50 (2.00–5.00) | **227 ± 26.6** (n=5) | 130 ± 36.9 (n=5) |

  - [[sources/pmda-review-report-erleada-apalutamide-hybrid|PMDA Review Report]] [PMDA-RR-Erleada | Review Report | Section 6.2.1.1 Study 21 Table 18 | p.34]
  - Verbatim: "240 | Apalutamide | 6 | 3.12 ± 0.745 | 3.50 (2.00, 5.00) | 227 ± 26.6*2 | 130 ± 36.9*2"

- **Dose proportionality (Japanese):** Cmax and AUCinf of both apalutamide and M3 (N-desmethyl) increased almost dose-proportionally over 60–240 mg in Japanese subjects — concordant with the foreign Phase I/II Study 001 dose-escalation results.
  - Verbatim: "Cmax and AUCinf of apalutamide and M3 increased in an almost dose-proportional manner in the dose range investigated."

### Japanese mCRPC patient steady-state PK — Study 08 (CTD 5.3.5.2.2; PCR1008; ongoing from July 2014)

Open-label, N=6 Japanese metastatic CRPC patients, soft capsule (later tablet), 240 mg QD with ADT.

- **Steady-state Cmax (Day 22 of Cycle 1):** **7.57 µg/mL**.
- **Steady-state AUC24h (Day 22 of Cycle 1):** **122 µg·h/mL**.
- **Accumulation index (Day 22 / Day −7):** **3.55** (arithmetic mean).
- **Steady state reached:** Week 4 (plasma trough plateaued).
  - [[sources/pmda-review-report-erleada-apalutamide-hybrid|PMDA Review Report]] [PMDA-RR-Erleada | Review Report | Section 6.2.1.2 Study 08 footnote 22 | p.27]
  - Verbatim: "Cmax and AUC24h on Day 22 of Cycle 1 in Japanese patients with metastatic CRPC orally receiving apalutamide 240 mg QD in Study 08 were 7.57 μg/mL and 122 μg∙h/mL, respectively"
- **Unbound fraction in humans:** 0.042 (used to compute unbound Cmax for safety-margin calculations against rat exposures).
  - [[sources/pmda-review-report-erleada-apalutamide-hybrid|PMDA Review Report]] [PMDA-RR-Erleada | Review Report | Section 5.7 toxicity footnote 6 | p.14]
  - Verbatim: "Cmax (7.57 µg/mL)... and unbound fraction (0.042) in humans"

### PMDA conclusion on Japanese vs non-Japanese PK

- **PMDA's overall assessment:** "No clear differences were observed in exposure (Cmax and AUC24h) to apalutamide after the oral administration of 240 mg as a single dose or QD between the Japanese phase I study (Study 08) and the phase I part of a foreign phase I/II study (Study 001). Thus, there was no difference in the PK of apalutamide between Japanese and non-Japanese patients."
  - [[sources/pmda-review-report-erleada-apalutamide-hybrid|PMDA Review Report]] [PMDA-RR-Erleada | Review Report | Section 6.2.9 Japanese vs non-Japanese | p.40]
- **Reconciliation with EMA population-PK finding:** EMA's pop-PK Japanese-vs-White covariate analysis gave AUC GMR 1.14 (90% CI 1.08, 1.20) in subjects N=58 vs 761 — a modest 14% Japanese-higher signal. PMDA's bilateral 240 mg comparison (Study 08 vs Study 001 Phase I N≈12 each) showed "no clear difference". Both views are valid: at the population scale the modest difference is statistically detectable; at the per-study level it's below the noise floor. Neither warrants Japanese-specific dose adjustment.

### Population PK refinement (PMDA-reviewed pooled analysis)

- **Pooled dataset:** 1092 subjects, 8579 apalutamide concentrations + 7944 M3 concentrations from Japanese (Studies 08 + 21) + global (Study 003) + foreign Phase I (Studies 11, 18, 19, 001).
- **Selected significant covariates:** body weight + serum albumin + health status (for F); body weight (for Vp/F); health status (for Qm/F of M3).
- **Healthy-vs-patient differential (PMDA value, body-weight + albumin corrected):** F in healthy adults is **27% higher** than in CRPC patients — independently confirms the EMA pop-PK estimate.
- **Soft-capsule vs FC tablet absorption rate:** Ka for soft capsule was estimated **14.9% lower** than for FC tablet — explains why capsule and tablet are "almost BE" rather than strictly BE.
  - [[sources/pmda-review-report-erleada-apalutamide-hybrid|PMDA Review Report]] [PMDA-RR-Erleada | Review Report | Section 6.2.7 PPK | p.40]
  - Verbatim: "The Ka value for soft capsule was estimated to be 14.9% lower than that for FC tablet."
