# Special populations — apalutamide

The EMA EPAR Public Assessment Report Section 2.4.2 provides the most comprehensive subgroup analysis: a forest-plot of population-PK-derived AUC GMRs across covariates (N=1092 in the pooled pop-PK dataset combining studies 001, 1008, 1011, 1018, 1019, 1021, and 003/SPARTAN).

## Hepatic impairment

- **Mild or moderate hepatic impairment vs normal (population PK):** AUC GMR 1.02 (90% CI 0.98, 1.05), N=118 vs 974 — **no clinically relevant effect on apalutamide AUC**.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Population PK covariates | p.49]
  - Verbatim: "Hepatic Impairment | Mild or moderate vs. Normal | 1.02 (0.98 to 1.05); N = 118 vs. 974"
- **Dedicated hepatic-impairment study 1018 (single-dose 240 mg tablet, parallel):** Mean Cmax — normal N=8: 1.95 µg/mL (SD 0.428); mild HI N=8: 2.09 µg/mL (SD 0.948); moderate HI N=8: 2.23 µg/mL (SD 1.17). Geomean AUC essentially overlapping (203 / 196 / 253 µg·h/mL).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Multidisciplinary Review | Section 19 — Table 47 study 1018 | p.175]
  - Verbatim: "240 | tablet | 8 | 3.00 | (1.0-3.0) | 1.95 | (0.428) | 203 | (37.4) | 191 | (53.6) | 1018"
- **Severe hepatic impairment:** Not studied — no PK data in severely impaired subjects (Child-Pugh C); use caution.

## Renal impairment

- **Mild renal impairment vs normal (population PK):** AUC GMR 1.03 (90% CI 1.00, 1.05), N=453 vs 372 — clinically negligible.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Population PK covariates | p.49]
  - Verbatim: "Renal Impairment | Mid vs. Normal | 1.03 (1.00 to 1.05); N = 453 vs. 372"
- **Moderate or severe renal impairment vs normal (population PK):** AUC GMR 1.08 (90% CI 1.04, 1.13), N=132 vs 372 — modest ~8% increase; not clinically meaningful at 240 mg QD.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Population PK covariates | p.49]
  - Verbatim: "Renal Impairment | Moderate or severe vs. Normal | 1.08 (1.04 to 1.13); N = 132 vs. 372"
- **End-stage renal disease / dialysis:** Not studied.
- **Mechanism:** Renal excretion of unchanged drug is only 1.2% of dose ([[evidence/pharmacokinetics]]), so renal impairment is expected to have minimal impact on apalutamide clearance — confirmed by the pop-PK analysis above.

- **Age <65 vs 65–75 (population PK):** AUC GMR 1.00 (90% CI 0.97, 1.04), N=273 vs 420 — clinically negligible.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Population PK covariates | p.49]

## Body weight

- **Body weight <75 kg vs 75–95 kg (population PK):** AUC GMR 1.09 (90% CI 1.05, 1.12), N=279 vs 504.
- **Body weight >95 kg vs 75–95 kg (population PK):** AUC GMR 0.89 (90% CI 0.87, 0.92), N=295 vs 504.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Population PK covariates | p.49]

## Race / ethnicity

- **Japanese vs White (population PK):** AUC GMR 1.14 (90% CI 1.08, 1.20), N=58 vs 761 — modest 14% increase; not warranting Japanese-specific dose adjustment.
- **Asian non-Japanese vs White:** GMR 1.06 (90% CI 1.01, 1.12), N=58 vs 761.
- **Black vs White:** GMR 1.03 (90% CI 0.98, 1.08), N=71 vs 761 — negligible.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Population PK covariates | p.49]

- **Dedicated Japanese-CRPC study 1021:** Japanese patients at 240 mg multiple-dose tablet (N=61) mean Cmax 3.12 µg/mL (SD 0.745), AUC 227 µg·h/mL (SD 26.6) — consistent with non-Japanese exposure.

## Health status — healthy subjects vs cancer patients

- **Healthy subjects have 27–42% higher apalutamide AUC than CRPC patients:** Population-PK estimated GMR 1.42 (90% CI 1.36, 1.49) for apalutamide and 1.29 (90% CI 1.25, 1.33) for N-desmethyl apalutamide (N=117 vs 975).
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Health Status | p.47]
  - Verbatim: "Health status Healthy Subject vs. Cancer Patient | GMR (90% CI): Sample size: 1.42 (1,36 to 1.49); N = 117 vs. 975"
- **Implication:** Single-dose PK measured in healthy volunteers (e.g. studies 1011 BE, 1012 DDI inhibitors) likely **over-predicts** patient exposure by ~30–40%. CHMP accepted this caveat after the applicant's discussion; the differential is partially explained by other confounders (concomitant medications, comorbidities, age) but is the main reason healthy-vs-patient PK extrapolation requires care.

## Polymorphism / pharmacogenomics

- **CYP2C8 poor-metaboliser status:** Not expected to clinically affect apalutamide PK or alter DDI predictions with CYP3A4 inhibitors. CHMP-accepted after sponsor discussion.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Polymorphism | p.46]
  - Verbatim: "the interaction of apalutamide with CYP3A4 inhibitors in CYP2C8 poor metabolisers is expected to be similar as in other genotypes"

## Sex

- Apalutamide is indicated only in men with prostate cancer; female subjects were not studied. No sex-stratified PK is available or anticipated.

## Pediatrics

- Not indicated. No pediatric PK studies.

## Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C) — new dedicated study (SUPPL_021, 2025)

A dedicated severe hepatic impairment PK study was conducted post-approval and submitted as NDA 210951/S-021, approved September 2025. The original NDA's study 1018 was limited to mild and moderate impairment.

- **AUC for active moieties** (sum of unbound apalutamide + potency-adjusted unbound N-desmethyl apalutamide) is **2.1-fold increased** in subjects with severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C) compared to subjects with normal hepatic function.
  - [[sources/suppl-021-210951s021lbl-hybrid|FDA Supplement S-021 Labeling]] [NDA-210951-S021-LBL | Labeling | Clinical Pharmacology - Hepatic Impairment | p.15]
  - Verbatim: "In subjects with severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C), AUC for the active moieties (sum of unbound apalutamide plus the potency-adjusted unbound N-desmethyl apalutamide) increased 2.1-fold compared to subjects with normal hepatic function."
- **Updated dose recommendation for severe HI:** **120 mg orally once daily** (50% reduction from standard 240 mg).
  - [[sources/suppl-021-210951s021lbl-hybrid|FDA Supplement S-021 Labeling]] [NDA-210951-S021-LBL | Labeling | Section 2.3 Recommended Dosage | p.2]
  - Verbatim: "The recommended dosage of ERLEADA for patients with severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class C) is 120 mg orally once daily"
- **Mild and moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh A/B):** Confirmed — no clinically significant differences vs normal; no dose adjustment.
  - Verbatim: "In subjects with mild to moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh A to B), no clinically significant differences in the pharmacokinetics of apalutamide or N-desmethyl apalutamide were observed compared to subjects with normal hepatic function."

## Renal impairment (label re-confirmation, SUPPL_021 2025)

- **eGFR 30–89 mL/min/1.73 m² (mild + moderate):** No clinically significant differences in apalutamide or N-desmethyl PK.
  - [[sources/suppl-021-210951s021lbl-hybrid|FDA Supplement S-021 Labeling]] [NDA-210951-S021-LBL | Labeling | Specific Populations | p.15]
  - Verbatim: "No clinically significant differences in the pharmacokinetics of apalutamide or N-desmethyl apalutamide were observed based on age (18-94 years), race (Black, non-Japanese Asian, Japanese), or eGFR 30-89 mL/min/1.73 m2"
- **eGFR ≤29 mL/min/1.73 m² (severe renal impairment + ESRD):** Effect on PK is **unknown** — no PK study in this population.
  - [[sources/suppl-021-210951s021lbl-hybrid|FDA Supplement S-021 Labeling]] [NDA-210951-S021-LBL | Labeling | Specific Populations | p.15]
  - Verbatim: "The effect of eGFR ≤29 mL/min/1.73 m2 on apalutamide pharmacokinetics is unknown."

### Key observations

1. **Mild and moderate HI:** Apalutamide AUC and Cmax remain within 0.95–1.13 of normal — no clinically relevant effect on the parent (confirms FDA + EMA conclusions).
2. **N-desmethyl in moderate HI is 19–27% LOWER** (Cmax GMR 0.73, AUC GMR 0.81) — likely because hepatic metabolism that *produces* N-desmethyl is reduced in moderate HI. Clinical relevance: small (N-desmethyl contributes ~28-32% of clinical activity per EMA).
3. **Unbound form shows the same pattern as total form** — protein binding is not appreciably altered by mild/moderate HI.
4. **Severe HI** is not in Study 18 — that's the population the post-approval FDA S-021 (2025) finally addressed (see Severe HI section above).

## Renal impairment — adverse-event subgroup analysis from SPARTAN (PMDA Section 6.2.5)

PMDA reviewed adverse-event rates in SPARTAN by renal function subgroup as supporting evidence that no dose adjustment is needed for renal impairment.

| Renal function | n | All adverse events | Grade ≥3 adverse events |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal (CrCL ≥90) | 377 | 96.0% | 43.0% |
| Mild RI (CrCL 60–89) | 280 | 97.1% | 47.5% |
| Moderate or severe RI (CrCL <60, n=137 + 4) | 141 | 96.5% | 46.8% |

  - [[sources/pmda-review-report-erleada-apalutamide-hybrid|PMDA Review Report]] [PMDA-RR-Erleada | Review Report | Section 6.2.5 Renal impairment | p.39]
  - Verbatim: "patients with normal renal function (n = 377), patients with mild renal impairment (n = 280), and patients with moderate or severe renal impairment (n = 141) were (a) 96.0%, 97.1%, and 96.5%, and (b) 43.0%, 47.5%, and 46.8%, respectively"
- **PMDA conclusion:** No dose adjustment needed for renal impairment — based on negligible renal excretion of unchanged apalutamide (1.2% of dose) plus the comparable subgroup AE rates above.
