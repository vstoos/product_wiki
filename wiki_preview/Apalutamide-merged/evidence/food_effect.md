# Food effect — apalutamide

## Tablet (to-be-market) formulation — Study 1011

Parallel-group design: N=15 fasted, N=15 fed, single 240 mg dose (4×60 mg tablets), open-label randomized.

- **Apalutamide Cmax (fasted vs fed GMR, 90% CI):** 0.84 (0.75, 0.94) — Cmax decreased ~16% with high-fat meal (statistically distinguishable, not clinically relevant per FDA reviewer).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Food Effect | p.75]
  - Verbatim: "Cmax | E | 15 | 2.01 | 83.95 | (74.90, 94.11) | 18.5%"
- **Apalutamide AUClast (fasted vs fed GMR, 90% CI):** 0.97 (0.86, 1.09) — no clinically relevant change.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Food Effect | p.75]
  - Verbatim: "AUClast | (µg.h/mL) | E | 15 | 206.53 | 96.73 | (85.76, 109.10)"
- **Apalutamide AUCinf (fasted vs fed GMR, 90% CI):** 0.95 (0.84, 1.06) — no clinically relevant change.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Food Effect | p.75]
  - Verbatim: "AUC .. | (µg.h/mL) | E | 14 | 209.78 | 94.08 | (83.75, 105.70)"
- **Tmax shift:** Median tmax delayed ~2 hours with food.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Food Effect | p.74]
  - Verbatim: "Median tmax was delayed about 2 hours with food"

## Capsule formulation — Study 001 substudy

2-way crossover, N=12 CRPC patients at steady-state, Days 1 and 15 of Cycle 3.

- **Apalutamide Cmax (fasted/fed GMR, 90% CI):** 0.86 (0.76, 0.96) — Cmax slightly higher fasted; not clinically relevant.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Food Effect Capsule | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide | 0.86 (0.76, 0.96) | 0.95 (0.89, 1.02) | NA"
- **Apalutamide AUC0-24 (fasted/fed GMR, 90% CI):** 0.95 (0.89, 1.02).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Food Effect Capsule | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide | 0.86 (0.76, 0.96) | 0.95 (0.89, 1.02) | NA"
- **N-desmethyl Cmax (fasted/fed GMR, 90% CI):** 0.95 (0.87, 1.05).
- **N-desmethyl AUC0-24 (fasted/fed GMR, 90% CI):** 1.01 (0.97, 1.06).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Food Effect Capsule | p.66]
  - Verbatim: "N-desmethyl apalutamide | 0.95 (0.87, 1.05) | 1.01 (0.97, 1.06) | NA"

## Clinical conclusion

- **Reviewer conclusion:** No clinically relevant food effect; ERLEADA may be taken with or without food.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — Food Effect | p.74]
  - Verbatim: "There was no clinically relevant exposure difference in fed and fasted conditions."

## 240 mg single-tablet (G043) food effect — Study PCR1028 Part 2 (post-approval)

A new food-effect study on the modern 240 mg single-tablet formulation (G043) was conducted in healthy male volunteers (N=20, 2×2 crossover). Submitted with EMA Extension X/0028/G (2023) and incorporated into the updated label.

- **Apalutamide Cmax (fed/fasted GMR, 90% CI):** **0.91 (0.83, 1.00)** — ~9% Cmax decrease with high-fat meal (vs ~16% with the 4×60 mg G023 in the original Study 1011). Within 80–125% BE.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 2.6.3 — Study 1028 Part 2 Table 6 | p.28]
  - Verbatim: "Cmax (ug/mL) | 2.36 | 2.15 | 90.96 | 83.06-99.61 | 16.7"
- **Apalutamide AUC0-72h (fed/fasted GMR, 90% CI):** **1.00 (0.97, 1.03)** — essentially no food effect on total exposure.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 2.6.3 — Study 1028 Part 2 Table 6 | p.28]
  - Verbatim: "AUC0-72h (h*ug/mL) | 59.2 | 59.2 | 99.94 | 97.12-102.84 | 5.2"
- **Intra-participant CV (Cmax):** 16.7% (measured in this crossover).
- **Intra-participant CV (AUC0-72h):** 5.2% (measured in this crossover).
- **CHMP conclusion:** "the bioavailability of the new strength formulation is not affected by the concomitant intake of food and can be taken irrespective of meals, like the currently marketed strength."
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 2.6.3 Discussion on Clinical Pharmacology | p.30]
- **Comparison to original food effect on 4×60 mg tablet (Study 1011):** Cmax GMR fed/fasted in old G023 4-tablet was 0.84 (0.75–0.94); in new G043 single tablet it is 0.91 (0.83–1.00). The newer single-tablet formulation appears to have a slightly attenuated food effect on Cmax — consistent with a different in-vivo dissolution surface area (1 large tablet vs 4 small) but small magnitude difference; neither effect is clinically relevant.
