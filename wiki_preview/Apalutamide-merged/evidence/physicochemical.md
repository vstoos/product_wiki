## Acid-base / ionization

- **pKa (acidic carboxamide moiety):** 9.7.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Chemistry/Product Quality Review | Drug Substance | p.7]
  - Verbatim: "The drug substance has a dissociation constant pKa of 9.7 (acidic carboxamide moiety)."
- **Ionizable at physiological pH:** No — apalutamide is not ionizable at relevant physiological pH; acid-lowering agents (PPI, H2RA, antacids) are not expected to alter bioavailability.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Multidisciplinary Review | Clinical Pharmacology — Effect of pH on Apalutamide | p.72]
  - Verbatim: "Apalutamide is not ionizable under relevant physiological pH condition. Acid lowering agents (e.g. proton pump inhibitor, H2-receptor antagonist, antacid) are not expected to affect the bioavailability of apalutamide."

  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Chemistry/Product Quality Review | Drug Substance | p.7]

- **In vitro permeability (Caco-2 Papp A-to-B):** 42.3 × 10⁻⁶ cm/s — supports BCS Class 2 high-permeability designation.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Absorption | p.42]
  - Verbatim: "The in vitro permeability of MDV3100 is high (Papp A-to-B of 42.3x10-6 cm/s)"
- **Aqueous solubility (CHMP quantitative value):** 0.001 g/100 mL = ~0.01 mg/mL = 2.5 mg per 250 mL (the FDA BCS solubility-classification volume) — comfortably below the 250-mg-in-250-mL threshold for "high solubility" at the maximum 240 mg dose; reinforces BCS Class 2 low solubility designation.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Absorption | p.42]
