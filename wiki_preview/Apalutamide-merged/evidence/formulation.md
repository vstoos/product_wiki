# Formulation — apalutamide

## Drug product description

- **Dosage form / strength / route:** Film-coated tablet, 60 mg apalutamide (free base), oral. Tablets are oblong, slightly yellowish to greyish green, debossed with "AR 60" on one side.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Chemistry/Product Quality Review | Description | p.12]
  - Verbatim: "ERLEADA (apalutamide) is supplied as film-coated tablets for oral administration containing 60 mg of apalutamide."
- **Recommended dose:** 240 mg (four 60 mg tablets) once daily.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Product Overview — Recommended Dose | p.6]
  - Verbatim: "ERLEADA 240 mg (four 60 mg tablets) administered orally once daily."

## Composition — core tablet

- **Inactive ingredients (core):** colloidal anhydrous silica, croscarmellose sodium, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose-acetate succinate (HPMC-AS), magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, silicified microcrystalline cellulose.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Description | p.12]
  - Verbatim: "Inactive ingredients of the core tablet are: colloidal anhydrous silica, croscarmellose sodium, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose-acetate succinate, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, and silicified microcrystalline cellulose."

## Composition — film coat

- **Film-coat excipients:** iron oxide black, iron oxide yellow, polyethylene glycol, polyvinyl alcohol, talc, titanium dioxide.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Description | p.12]
  - Verbatim: "The tablets are finished with a commercially available film-coating consisting of the following excipients: iron oxide black, iron oxide yellow, polyethylene glycol, polyvinyl alcohol, talc, and titanium dioxide."

## Container closure & storage

- **Packaging:** 160 cc HDPE bottles, 120 tablets per bottle, with silica gel desiccant.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Drug Product | p.7]
  - Verbatim: "The drug product is packaged in 160 cc HDPE bottles containing 120 count tablets with a silica gel desiccant"
- **Shelf life:** 24 months at 20–25 °C (excursions 15–30 °C).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Executive Summary — Recommendations | p.6]
  - Verbatim: "A shelf life of 24 months is granted for ERLEADA (apalutamide) tablets stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F)"

## Capsule-to-tablet bridging history

The to-be-market 60 mg tablet replaced an earlier 30 mg capsule used in Phase 1/2; relative BE was demonstrated via Study 1011 — see [[evidence/bioequivalence]].

## EMA EPAR multi-source confirmation

- **Tablet formulation chosen for commercialization:** Spray-Dried Powder (SDP) with HPMC-AS at a 1:3 drug:polymer ratio — a solid amorphous dispersion specifically designed to overcome apalutamide's BCS Class 2 low aqueous solubility.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Bioequivalence | p.42]
  - Verbatim: "B: 240 mg dose given as 4x60 mg SDP (HPMC-AS ratio 1:3) tablets"
- **Soft-gel capsule history:** Early Phase 1/2 studies used "liquid-filled soft gelatin capsules containing a non-aqueous lipid-based solution of apalutamide" — a SEDDS-class delivery system that achieved comparable AUC to the final SDP tablet.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Bioequivalence | p.42]
  - Verbatim: "Early clinical pharmacology and clinical studies used liquid-filled soft gelatin capsules containing a non-aqueous lipid-based solution of apalutamide"
- **HME tablet alternative tested:** Hot-Melt Extrusion (HME) tablet at HPMC-AS 1:3 (Treatment C in Study 1011) was tested as an alternative process; Cmax GMR vs capsule 84.90% (90% CI 74.59, 96.65) — slightly lower bioavailability than the chosen SDP tablet.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-EPAR | EPAR Assessment | Section 2.4.2 — Bioequivalence Table 5 | p.42]
- **SDP/Eudragit alternative tested:** SDP at Eudragit 1:2 (Treatment D) Cmax GMR vs capsule 95.61% (90% CI 83.99, 108.83) — comparable to chosen SDP/HPMC-AS but Eudragit polymer was not the commercial choice.

## 240 mg single-tablet formulation (G043) — post-approval addition

A new single-tablet 240 mg strength of apalutamide was approved via FDA NDA 210951/S-011 (2022) and EMA Extension X/0028/G (2023) to reduce pill burden from 4 tablets to 1 per daily dose.

- **240 mg tablet (G043) physical description:** Oval shaped, bluish grey to grey film-coated tablet of approximately 10 mm × 21 mm dimensions, debossed with "E240" on one side, containing 240 mg of apalutamide.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 2.2.3 Finished Product | p.6]
  - Verbatim: "The finished product is oval shaped, bluish grey to grey film-coated 10 mm width and 21 mm length for oral administration, debossed with \"E240\" on one side and contains 240 mg of apalutamide."
- **Differentiation from 60 mg tablet:** The 60 mg strength (G023) is oblong, greenish film-coated ~9 mm × 17 mm with "AR 60" debossing. Both colour, shape, and debossing distinguish the strengths.
  - Same source.
- **Composition difference:** The 240 mg G043 composition is similar to the 60 mg G023 but **does not contain microcrystalline cellulose**.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 2.2.3 Composition | p.6]
  - Verbatim: "The composition of the 240 mg strength is similar to the 60 mg film-coated tablet but does not contain cellulose microcrystalline."
- **Bioequivalence:** G043 240 mg single-tablet **is bioequivalent** to 4×60 mg G023 (PCR1028 Cmax GMR 109.67%, 90% CI 104.55–115.04%; AUC0-72h GMR 102.71%, 90% CI 100.78–104.68%). See [[evidence/bioequivalence]] §"Post-approval BE studies".
- **Shelf life:** 24 months under long-term ICH conditions (25 °C/60% RH), in HDPE bottles or blisters.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-004452-x-0028-g-epar-assessment-report-extension-hybrid|EMA EPAR Extension X/0028/G]] [EMEA-H-C-004452-X-0028-G | EPAR Assessment - Extension | Section 2.2.5 Stability | p.~12]
- **Manufacturing intermediate:** Same active substance (apalutamide drug substance) as the 60 mg strength; finished product made from an intermediate delivering 240 mg per tablet.
