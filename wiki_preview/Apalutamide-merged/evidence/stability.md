# Stability — apalutamide

- **Drug product (commercial container, 20–25 °C):** Stable for 24 months.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Executive Summary — Recommendations | p.6]
  - Verbatim: "A shelf life of 24 months is granted for ERLEADA (apalutamide) tablets stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F)"
- **Excursion conditions:** 15–30 °C permitted (USP Controlled Room Temperature).
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | Executive Summary | p.6]
  - Verbatim: "excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature]"

## Storage conditions for the patient

- **Patient-facing storage:** Original package, protect from light/moisture, do not discard desiccant.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000chemr-hybrid|FDA Chemistry Review]] [NDA-210951-ChemR | How Supplied/Storage and Handling | p.13]
  - Verbatim: "Store in the original package. Do not discard desiccant. Protect from light and moisture."
