# Apalutamide — Clinical Studies

The apalutamide NDA 210951 submission included **one Phase 3 pivotal efficacy trial** (SPARTAN), **one Phase 1/2 dose-escalation trial**, **one dedicated QT study in patients**, and **eleven Phase 1 clinical-pharmacology / biopharmaceutic studies** (mostly in healthy male subjects). The EMA Variation II/0001 dossier added the Phase 3 **TITAN** trial supporting the metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC) indication extension (now extracted). The EMA Extension X/0028/G added two BE studies supporting the 240 mg single-tablet strength. The FDA S-021 supplement (2025-09) added new severe-hepatic-impairment study data. See [[post_approval_variations]] for the complete post-approval timeline.

> Studies marked **[study page]** have a dedicated `studies/clinical/<slug>.md`. Studies marked **[evidence only]** contribute facts to the evidence pages but do not yet have a dedicated page; their per-study extraction is scheduled for the next pass. Verbatim study identifiers were extracted from FDA Multidisciplinary Review Section 6.3.1 (p.171) — see "Source verbatim" at the bottom.
