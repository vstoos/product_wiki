# TITAN — apalutamide Phase 3 in mHSPC

## Identifiers

- **Canonical id:** `nct02489318`
- **NCT:** NCT02489318
- **Sponsor code:** 56021927PCR3002 (also referred to as PCR3002 or Study 3002)
- **Nickname:** TITAN

## Overview

TITAN was a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 study of apalutamide + ADT vs placebo + ADT in **1052 patients** (525 apalutamide + 527 placebo; 1 randomized subject not treated, so 1051 in safety population) with metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC). Dual primary endpoints: **radiographic progression-free survival (rPFS)** and **overall survival (OS)**.

## Trial design

- **Treatment:** Apalutamide 240 mg orally daily + ADT vs placebo + ADT.
- **Population:** Men ≥18 years with mHSPC (low or high volume).
- **Stratification factors:** Gleason score at diagnosis (>7 vs ≤7), prior docetaxel use (yes vs no), region (Other Countries vs North America and European Union).
- **Sample size:** 1052 randomized (525 apalutamide + 527 placebo); 1052 ITT; 1051 safety population (1 randomized but not treated, excluded).
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.4.2.1 — Study description Table 1 | p.9]
  - Verbatim: "In Study 56021927PCR3002, 1052 subjects were randomized, but 1 did not receive treatment, resulting in 1051 subjects in the safety population and 1052 subjects in the intent-to-treat (ITT) population"

## Primary endpoint results (initial analysis, 22 Nov 2018 data cutoff)

### Radiographic progression-free survival (rPFS)

- **rPFS hazard ratio:** **0.50** (95% CI 0.40, 0.62) — apalutamide+ADT vs ADT alone.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.4.3 Exposure-Response | p.18]
  - Verbatim: "The observed apalutamide exposure levels following the administration of apalutamide 240 mg once daily with ADT was shown to be efficacious in extending the rPFS (hazard ratio [HR]=0.50; 95% CI: 0.40, 0.62)"
- **24-month rPFS event-free rate:** ~68% apalutamide vs ~48% placebo.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.5.2 Efficacy Tables | p.~50]
  - Verbatim: "24-month event-free rate (95% CI) | 0.475 (0.421, 0.528) | 0.682 (0.629, 0.729)"

### Overall survival (OS) — initial analysis

- **OS hazard ratio (initial):** **0.69** (95% CI 0.52, 0.92) — apalutamide+ADT vs ADT alone.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.4.3 Exposure-Response | p.18]
  - Verbatim: "OS (HR=0.69; 95% CI: 0.52, 0.92) compared with ADT alone"

## Final OS analysis (SUPPL_006, 2021)

The fulfilled-PMC final OS analysis (TITAN survival with longer follow-up) was approved via FDA NDA 210951/S-006 in April 2021. Updated U.S. PI reflects this final analysis.

- **Source:** [[sources/suppl-006-210951orig1s006ltr-hybrid|FDA Supplement S-006 Approval Letter]] [NDA-210951-S006-LTR | Approval Letter | Letter body | p.1]
  - Verbatim: "This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application provides for updates in the U.S. prescribing information for Erleada (apalutamide) to reflect the analysis from the final report for Overall Survival (OS) of the TITAN trial."

## Subgroup analyses

- High-volume disease subgroup OS HR: 0.68 (95% CI 0.50, 0.92).
- Low-volume disease subgroup OS HR: 0.59 (95% CI 0.40, 0.89).
- Treatment effect consistent across Gleason score, region, and prior docetaxel subgroups (pre-specified).
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.5.2 Subgroup analyses | p.~75]

## Population pharmacokinetics (TITAN data)

- **Plasma concentrations analyzed:** 2,302 apalutamide and 2,303 N-desmethyl apalutamide concentrations from **501 subjects** with evaluable plasma PK data (apalutamide+ADT arm).
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.4.2.2 Pop PK | p.~13]
  - Verbatim: "In total, 2,302 apalutamide and 2,303 N-desmethyl apalutamide plasma concentrations from 501 subjects with evaluable plasma concentration-time data receiving 240 mg apalutamide each day in the apalutamide + ADT arm were used in the population PK analysis."
- **External-validation conclusion:** The PK from TITAN was consistent with the previously developed (SPARTAN/healthy-volunteer) population PK model. Inter-individual variability in TITAN was *slightly lower* than the model predicted.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.4.2.2 Pop PK | p.~14]
  - Verbatim: "The variability in plasma concentrations observed in Study 3002 (TITAN) was slightly lower than the model predicted variability"

## Exposure-response

- **rPFS:** No statistically significant exposure-response within the investigated range (240 mg QD). Multivariate Cox HR(AUC0-24) = 0.997 (95% CI 0.990, 1.004).
- **OS:** No statistically significant exposure-response. Multivariate Cox HR(AUC0-24) = 0.994 (95% CI 0.986, 1.003).
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.4.3 Exposure-Response | p.18]
  - Verbatim: "rPFS stratified HRAUC0-24=0.997 µg.h/mL; 95% CI: 0.990, 1.004 and OS stratified HRAUC0-24=0.994 µg.h/mL; 95% CI: 0.986, 1.003"

## DDI sub-study — apalutamide effect on leuprolide

A pre-planned PK substudy in TITAN evaluated co-administration with leuprolide (GnRH analogue, the ADT backbone).

- **Apparent leuprolide exposure 42% higher in apalutamide+leuprolide arm vs placebo+leuprolide.** Driven by higher pre-treatment baseline leuprolide rather than a pharmacokinetic DDI.
- **CHMP conclusion:** No clinically meaningful effect of apalutamide on steady-state leuprolide PK/PD.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.4.2 Leuprolide DDI | p.~22]
  - Verbatim: "Higher exposure of leuprolide (42%) was observed in the apalutamide+leuprolide arm versus placebo+leuprolide arm. This was caused by the higher exposure of leuprolide in patients at baseline (pre-treatment with apalutamide). Based on the available data, co-administration with apalutamide had no apparent effect on the steady-state exposure of leuprolide"

## Safety signals — cardiovascular

The TITAN data introduced an additional warning in EU labeling on **ischaemic cardiovascular events**; this was the basis for adding the warning to SmPC Section 4.4. See [[understanding/apalutamide-pharmacokinetics]] for the regulatory context.

- **QT signal:** No ventricular arrhythmias / QTc prolongation / torsade events reported in either TITAN arm. Combined incidence of syncope + loss of consciousness 1.0% apalutamide+ADT vs 0.9% placebo+ADT.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.4.3 Exposure-Response | p.20]
  - Verbatim: "In Study 3002 (TITAN), there were no adverse events associated with ventricular arrhythmias such as QTc prolongation, ventricular tachycardia, or torsade de pointes reported in either treatment arm."

## Quality of life

- **No statistically significant differences** between treatment groups on FACT-P or EQ5D-5L.
  - [[sources/erleada-erleada-h-c-4452-ii-0001-epar-assessment-report-variation-hybrid|EMA EPAR Variation II/0001]] [EMEA-H-C-4452-II-0001 | EPAR Assessment - Variation | Section 2.5.2 QoL | p.~58]
  - Verbatim: "There were no changes from baseline in the FACT-P in the apalutamide + ADT treatment arm (cycle 25, LS Means 0.50) and no differences compared to ADT (p=0.2367)."
