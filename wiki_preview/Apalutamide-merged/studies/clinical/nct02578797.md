# PCR1019 — apalutamide QT/QTc study

## Identifiers

- **Canonical id:** `nct02578797`
- **NCT:** NCT02578797
- **Sponsor code:** 56021927PCR1019

## Overview

Open-label, uncontrolled, multi-center, single-arm dedicated Phase 1b QT study in **45 patients** with castration-resistant prostate cancer at the therapeutic dose (apalutamide 240 mg QD). Measured time-matched ECG QTcF.

## Key results

- **Maximum mean ΔQTcF from baseline:** 12.4 ms (2-sided 90% upper CI 16.1 ms).
- **Exposure-QT relationship:** Concentration-dependent increase in QTcF for both apalutamide and N-desmethyl apalutamide.
  - [[sources/210951orig1s000multidiscipliner-hybrid|FDA Multidisciplinary Review]] [NDA-210951-MultiR | Clinical Pharmacology — QT Prolongation | p.68]
  - Verbatim: "The maximum mean QTcF change from baseline was 12.4 ms (2-sided 90% upper CI: 16.1 ms)."

## Labeling consequence

- ECG monitoring recommended in patients with CHF, bradyarrhythmias, drugs known to prolong QT (Class Ia/III antiarrhythmics), electrolyte abnormalities, severe hepatic impairment; correct hypoK/hypoMg before initiation. Avoid in patients with congenital long-QT syndrome.
