# ARN-509-001 — apalutamide first-in-patient Phase 1/2

## Identifiers

- **Canonical id:** `nct01171898`
- **NCT:** NCT01171898
- **Sponsor code:** ARN-509-001

## Overview

Open-label, dose-escalation, proof-of-concept study in 127 patients with progressive, advanced castration-resistant prostate cancer. Dose range 30–480 mg daily orally; MTD established at **240 mg QD** (which became the registered dose). Conducted at 18 US centers.

## Data by source

### From NDA 210951 Multidisciplinary Review (Table 9)

- **Trial design:** Open-label, dose-escalation, proof-of-concept.
- **Regimen:** Starting dose 30 mg QD; highest dose tested 480 mg QD; MTD 240 mg QD.
- **n enrolled:** 127.
- **Population:** Progressive, advanced CRPC.
- **Phase 2 endpoint:** PSA response at 12 weeks per PCWG2.
- **Centers / countries:** 18 / 1.
