# Understanding — apalutamide special populations

Limited variability evidence by subgroup is available in the publicly extracted FDA material so far ([[evidence/special_populations]]). The population PK analysis showed no meaningful effect on apalutamide PK at 240 mg QD from CYP2C8 inhibitor (n=53, 5%) or CYP3A4 inducer (n=24, 3%) co-medications — albeit with small subgroup sizes.

Dedicated hepatic-impairment and renal-impairment studies are summarized in the EMA Public Assessment Report ([[sources/erleada-erleada-epar-public-assessment-report-hybrid|EMA EPAR]]) and PMDA Review Report ([[sources/pmda-review-report-erleada-apalutamide-hybrid|PMDA RR]]); these sources are pending extraction in a subsequent ingest pass. Until those are extracted, the conservative position is:

- Apalutamide is heavily protein-bound (96%) and metabolized hepatically — moderate-to-severe hepatic impairment likely raises exposure.
- Renal excretion of unchanged drug is minimal (1.2% of dose) — renal impairment unlikely to materially affect apalutamide clearance, but the N-desmethyl metabolite is partly renally cleared and could accumulate.

These predictions should be confirmed against the EMA/PMDA dedicated studies when those are ingested.
